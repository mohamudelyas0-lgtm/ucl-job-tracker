About the Programme
This is a 2-year programme during which you'll have the opportunity to rotate into three different areas, beginning with two six-month placements in Marketing and Market Access, and then a twelve-month placement in Sales (field based). Each placement is designed to give you extraordinary insight into our business and you could be working on:
Marketing:
Build and develop multi-format, engaging content in line with the brand plan strategy using key insights of the local market
Work cross functionally to execute a brand plan and monitor impact of the campaign through insights
Build customer personas and their experience maps to develop an engagement strategy and plan
UK Market Access:
Supporting with a Health Technology Appraisal to the National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (NICE) or Scottish Medicines Consortium (SMC)
Planning and execution of market access to medicines
Independent research on topics such as the impact of COVID and Brexit on access to medicines
Health economic / budget impact modelling
Sales
Form relationships with customer base to increase prescribing of AstraZeneca brands through impactful clinical selling and influencing
Use detailed analysis to identify and evaluate opportunities to drive practice change
Work as a member of the account team to develop and implement effective account plans, achieve account objectives and important metrics through effective project management and collaboration
Relocation support may be offered to support a move out of London for the 3rd rotation and with access to a lease car (Sales rotation only).
As a member of the Association of the British Pharmaceutical Industry (ABPI), all of our sales, marketing and communications activities are subject to the ABPI Code of Practice, and you will start on your ABPI qualification as soon as you join us. Meanwhile our programme will provide you with the breadth of experience within the commercial business of a biopharmaceutical organisation to set you up for success in your career.
Essential Requirements
A degree (minimum 2.1) in a scientific (e.g. medical science) or business/economic subject area (e.g. health economics), either at an undergraduate (BSc) or postgraduate (Masters) level
Strong verbal and written communication skills
Ability to effectively communicate material through presentations and reports
The ability to work independently but also establish and maintain effective working relationships with co-workers
Basic skills in Microsoft Word, Excel and PowerPoint
Good understanding of the environment for pharmaceuticals in the UK, and/or eager to learn more
Ability to drive (Hold a clean UK driving licence) prior to application or have obtained your driving licence by the time you reach this rotation
