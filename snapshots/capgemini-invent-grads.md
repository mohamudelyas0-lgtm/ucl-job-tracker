Your future made real
You’ll reimagine what’s possible: creating value for the world’s leading organisations through technology to build a sustainable, more inclusive future. Working with a range of clients, all with a unique set of business, technological and societal ambitions, your work will make a real impact across the UK.
You will be part of one of the World’s Most Ethical Companies®, as recognised by Ethisphere®. Our seven values guide how we operate and how we make decisions, creating a culture built on trust, responsibility and collaboration.
You will also have the support to grow in the direction that is right for you. Whether you are developing new skills or exploring where your strengths lie, you will shape a career that evolves with you while helping businesses adapt and innovate in a changing world.

Values
Our Culture and Values are at the heart of Capgemini. We have 7 values and they define who we are, what we do and the way we work. The values have been in place since Capgemini’s founder, Serge Kampf, defined them in 1967. These will become part of your everyday in your role as an Executive, and you will be integral at maintaining the culture that sits at the centre of our work.
Learn more
Honesty
Honesty
Loyalty, integrity, uprightness, a total refusal to use any underhanded method to help win business or gain any kind of advantage
Boldness
Boldness
A flair for entrepreneurship, and a desire to take considered risks and show commitment.
Trust
Trust
The empowerment of both individuals and teams; to have decisions made as close as possible to where they will be put into practice.
Freedom
Freedom
Independence in thought and entrepreneurial spirit, creativity. It also means respect for others, different cultures and customs.
Fun
Fun
Feeling good about being part of the company or one’s team, feeling proud of what one does, feeling a sense of accomplishment.
Modesty
Modesty
Simplicity, the very opposite of affectation, pretension, pomposity, arrogance and boastfulness.
Team Spirit
Team Spirit
Solidarity, friendship, fidelity, generosity, fairness in sharing the benefits of collective work.
