Rewards & recognition
Beyond professional growth, we want Bioscript Group to be a place where people feel supported and rewarded for their work. Our competitive and broad benefits offering will continue to evolve, ensuring colleagues have access to the support and recognition they deserve. Today, our benefits include:
Time to recharge: generous holiday allowance, plus a full company shut-down over Christmas, so everyone can truly switch off.
Well-being cover: competitive, country-tailored health and wellness provision, plus an annual allowance to spend on whatever keeps you at your best.
Performance rewards: spot bonuses, individual recognition awards, team-wide awards, and long-service awards that honour sustained impact.
Remote-first flexibility: work where you do your best thinking, with the tech and trust to back you.
Purpose in action: a £50 birthday donation to a charity of your choice, plus one paid day each year to volunteer for any cause close to your heart.
Diversity, Equity & Inclusion
