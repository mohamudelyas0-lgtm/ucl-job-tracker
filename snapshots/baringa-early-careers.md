Develop your career from day one
We put people first and create impact that lasts. And we want to help you do the same. Baringa could be the start of a career as a capital markets advisor, energy trading specialist, vaccine supply chain expert, digital innovator and so much more. We want team players who are kind, curious and fuelled by the ambition to be great at what they do. Work alongside our industry experts and real clients from day one – from FTSE100 companies to bright new start-ups, and across every sector.

About the programme
8-week paid programme for university students in their penultimate year who want to get a taste of life as a consultant.
Applications for 2027 opportunities have now closed & will reopen next Autumn.
You must be able to work full-time during the programme.
You’ll have the right to work in the UK.
What we’re looking for
Well-rounded, talented people from all academic backgrounds.
Team players with a strong academic foundation.
You’ll be kind, curious, and with the ambition to be great at your work.
What you can expect
First-hand experience working at an award-winning consultancy firm
Direct experience on a client project with responsibility for delivering outcomes and real value for clients from day one.
To gain a solid understanding of the consulting business model, valuable experience across the project lifecycle, and new business analysis and client engagement techniques.
To work in a collaborative environment alongside industry experts and skilled professionals at the forefront of their fields.
What’s in it for you
A competitive salary and access to a salary advance.
Dedicated project manager who’ll give you formal support on your client project, providing you with coaching and mentoring throughout the programme.
Informal ‘buddy’ for you to ask general advice and (what you might think are) ‘silly questions’.
Interactive workshops and social events where you can get to know your colleagues personally and professionally – and learn more about Baringa, our clients, and our people.
The potential to secure a permanent position once you’ve finished your degree, joining about 3 out of every 4 interns who make the jump from intern to Graduate Analyst.
If successful, you’ll receive an Internship Completion Bonus when you join as an Analyst.
How to apply
Research our business units and choose the area where you want to try out life as a consultant. We’ll always do our best to match your preferences.
Download a pack with all the important dates, details about the application process, and what happens when you walk in the door. This is where you’ll discover more about how we work, our values and culture, our commitment to diversity, equality, and inclusion, how we act on sustainability and support the communities that mean the most to us.
