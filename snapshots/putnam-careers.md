Our evolution: Same team, working as one.
For decades, our businesses have served the health and life sciences industry to create real impact. Today, we reignite a new spark, evolving from Inizio Advisory to Inizio Ignite, bringing together our businesses – Putnam, Research Partnership, STEM, and Vynamic – to operate as one.
We are Inizio Ignite.
Our vast expertise across our teams allows us to tap into a greater level of depth and breath – an opportunity to cross–collaborate with scale. This evolution allows us to better serve you, our clients.
Together, we collaborate with you to ignite impact, accelerate performance, and deliver lasting change for patients. By uniting strategy, insights, and innovation with a unique depth and breadth of expertise, we drive transformation and shape the future of health.
About Inizio Ignite
Putnam.
Our Putnam strategy consulting team bridges science and strategy to enable confident decision-making and build value for pharma. We partner with leading health and life science companies serving brand & commercial leaders, Medical Affairs, access & pricing, and R&D. Our expertise spans offerings including:
Therapeutic area strategy
Data & AI strategy
Go-to-market strategy
Commercialization & growth
Value, pricing & access (VPA) strategy
Evidence generation
Vynamic.
Our Vynamic management consulting team connects strategy to action, driving healthcare transformation. We partner with leading health and life science companies serving brand & commercial, Medical affairs, R&D, and Health Services, our expertise spans offerings including:
Strategic planning & mobilization (SP&M)
Operating model & organization design (OMOD)
Deal activation & partnership enablement (DAPE)
Product launch strategy & planning
Customer experience transformation
Tech & digital transformation
Strategic project management office
Change management
Research Partnership.
Our Research Partnership healthcare market research team provides research and insights to inform and power healthcare decisions. Serving a variety of clients across the lifecycle, our expertise includes:
Data, research & insights (proprietary syndicated and custom research)
Brand tracking (ATU)
STEM.
Our STEM team uses benchmarking through AI-enabled and evidence-based approaches to drive alignment resulting in accelerated performance. Serving clients across brand & commercial, Medical Affairs, & Sales, our expertise includes:
Data, research & insights (proprietary benchmarking data)
Strategic brand alignment
Sales & MSL performance benchmarking

Some of our leaders:
Matt Riordan, Group President, Putnam
As President of Putnam, Matt leads a team focused on strategic acuity and patient impact. He has partnered with global biopharma across therapeutic areas from pipeline shaping to launch, applying structured thinking and objective analysis to drive results.
Dermot Kenny, Group President, STEM & Research Partnership
As President of STEM & Research Partnership, Dermot drives growth and innovation across the businesses. With more than 25 years in the pharmaceutical industry, he brings deep experience in clinical development, technology transformation, and digitization, advancing end-to-end platforms and innovation in clinical trial execution.
Haben Debessay, Group President, Vynamic
As President of Vynamic, Haben leads efforts to shape the future of healthcare through bold, strategic change. With more than two decades of experience in consulting and operational leadership, Haben helps clients and teams connect vision to execution—translating complex challenges into transformative outcomes that advance business performance.
Our solutions.
We help you to formulate your strategy, defining where to compete and how to succeed. This involves strategic planning and necessary transformations informed by deep expertise, research, and insights. We bridge the gap between ambition and execution, guiding tactical decisions and measuring performance against proprietary benchmarking data to ensure lasting success.
