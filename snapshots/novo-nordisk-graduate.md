EARLY CAREER PROGRAMMES
Our Graduate Programme
Apply from 16 Dec 2026 - 5 Jan 2027 for careers starting 1 Sep 2027
Explore the world while developing your career.
Welcome to Novo's Graduate Programme: a competitive, exciting experience at one of the world's largest healthcare companies.
You can apply from 16 December 2026 - 5 Jan 2027 for careers starting 1 September 2027!
For over 100 years, we have combined leading scientific expertise with a deep understanding of people's lives to help people live healthier lives. As we enter a new chapter, we're working with greater speed and ambition to increase the scale of our impact. Because we believe lasting health starts now.
To keep leading in diabetes and obesity care, we need people who bring new perspectives and stay curious. People who thrive on challenging what's possible and working together to win. People who want to join our shared ambition: to help millions of people live healthier lives. People like you.
And what can you expect from the Novo Graduate Programme? The opportunity to turn your master's degree into a meaningful career. Three rotations across the Novo value chain in locations across the world. Competitive compensation and benefits. Access to a broad range of learning and development opportunities. Mentorship from top senior leaders. The opportunity to build a strong international network. And the chance to launch a global career while helping shape the future of healthcare.
This year, we are hiring 50 graduates for 15 different tracks! Explore our opportunties below, apply from 16 December 2026 and kick-start your global career 1 September 2027. Please note that you can only apply for 1 programme.
Important: To apply, you'll need...
A relevant and recent master's degree (2026/2027) and excellent academic results
No more than one year of full time work experience between or after completing your master's degree
Availability to begin full time 1 September 2027
At least four months of previous international experience (through studying, working, or living abroad)
Professional fluency in English
The ability to travel globally over the two year programme
Read about the application process from now until first day at work.
Now, choose your track...
Novo offers multiple graduate programme tracks, which are suited to different academic backgrounds and interests. This year, we are hiring for 15 different tracks, which are anchored either in the Novo Headquarter in Denmark, or in some of our other offices around the world. All tracks include at least one rotation in a different location from the home office. Find the programme that fits your skills and passion. Read more about the different graduate tracks and locations below.
Three types of programmes: Some of our business areas offer multiple graduate tracks anchored in different locations.
Global tracks are anchored in our corporate headquarters in Copenhagen, Denmark.
International Operations (IO) tracks are anchored in some of our affiliate offices. For the 2027 recruitment cycle, IO tracks are hiring in Turkey, China, Australia, Singapore, and Denmark. Please note you must be able to legally work in the country location you apply for at the time of your application.
US Operations are anchored in our regional US headquarters in Princeton, New Jersey. Please note: Applications for the US Operations programme have now closed. Please return in 2028 to see the status of this hiring track
