Our Values
Lumanity is a values-driven organization where our core principles guide every aspect of our work.
Never settle
Excellence, expertise, conviction
In everything we do, we’re driven by a relentless pursuit of excellence. We invest in our skills and our talents to foster deep expertise and deliver exceptional work that will define our careers. Because for us, excellence is not just a goal — it’s the starting point.
Accelerate our impact
Impact, ambition, results
We are driven by our potential to deliver transformative impact. Lumanity is a place for those who dare to tackle big challenges and complex problems — those who want to make a difference. We inspire each other to reimagine what’s possible for ourselves, our clients, and patients.
Lead with authenticity
Integrity, diversity, belonging
Our power is in our differences. We cultivate a community where everyone can be their authentic self. We’re “people people” — we celebrate our unique perspectives and always treat each other with respect. We are able to do our best work because diversity is at the core of who we are.
Be changemakers
Innovation, creativity, courage
We are curious explorers and lifelong learners. To ensure our work has true impact, we challenge old ways of thinking. We see what others don’t and stay one step ahead. Because we know that innovation can be found through the unconventional, the daring, the visionary.
Connect the dots
Teamwork, partnership, one Lumanity
We value and foster partnership, bringing the right mix of specialties for each project to solve the most important challenges. When we come together, we’re greater than the sum of our parts. Our unique perspectives enable us to form true partnerships — with each other and our clients.
We value your well-being
We know that you thrive when you have the right support and resources. Our rewards reflect our inclusive values; browse the benefits you can look forward to:
Health & Wellness
With comprehensive healthcare and life insurance packages, we’ve got you covered.
Heart & Mind
We value your emotional health and offer a variety of programs to support your mental healthcare.
Family
Our medical programs cover your entire family; we also offer maternity, paternity, and adoption leave.
Financial Health
We offer generous compensation packages, industry-leading retirement plans, and financial planning education to help you meet your savings goals.
Growth
We help feed our greatest asset (your minds!) through our learning and development programs, coaching, career planning, and tuition reimbursement.
Giving
It feels good to pay it forward, and we support that through organized volunteer programs, company matching incentives, and paid time to participate.
What matters to us
Everyone in
We have long standing commitments to diversity, equity, and inclusion. It not only enables us to better serve our clients, it is core to who we are. We strive to attract and retain talented changemakers from all backgrounds and foster a culture where you can be you. We lift up internal champions and bring in external experts to provide learning sessions, foster forum discussions, and celebrate our differences.
Learn more about diversity, equity, and inclusion
Our responsibilities reach far
Corporate social responsibility is deeply important to us. We do big things for our clients, and we help them improve patient outcomes all over the world. We believe that our planet matters and have a detailed plan. We believe it is better to give than to receive and provide you with resources and time to make a positive impact in your community. We look after each other by creating communities and programs to strengthen our wellbeing, both physically and mentally.
Learn more about social responsibility
A growth mindset
We invest in you, and your career development at Lumanity. We not only provide programs to find mentors, take classes, lead new projects, we encourage and incentivize it. Our leadership looks for opportunities to lean in and reach out to create diverse teams with different perspectives and experiences to enable professional growth while at the same time creating a better end solution for our clients.
