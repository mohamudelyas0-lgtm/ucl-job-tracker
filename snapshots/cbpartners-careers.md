Fraudulent Recruiting Activity Notice
Trinity (aka Trinity Life Sciences) is aware of fraudulent recruiting activity conducted by individuals falsely claiming to represent our company and using email addresses associated with the trinitylifescienceshr.com domain.
Please be advised that Trinity will never ask job applicants to pay fees, purchase equipment, provide banking information, or send money at any stage of the recruiting or hiring process.
All legitimate employment opportunities are posted exclusively on our official Careers page. Recruitment-related emails from Trinity will only be sent from authorized company email addresses ending in trinitylifesciences.com.
If you receive a suspicious email, text message, or other communication claiming to be from Trinity—particularly one using the trinitylifescienceshr.com domain—do not respond, click links, or provide personal or financial information.
If you believe you have been targeted by a recruiting scam, report the incident to your local law-enforcement agency, the platform on which the communication originated, and the Federal Trade Commission at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.
