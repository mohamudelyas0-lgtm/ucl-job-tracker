Graduate hires
As a new consultant in our practice you will help our teams deliver real projects, train with a growing community of talented professionals and contribute to the success of our business from day one.
Working across our strategy practice, you will learn how to help our clients solve their most critical challenges from developing new strategies and undertaking due diligence to supporting major strategic decisions.
We offer a competitive starting salary and a generous performance bonus and profit share, reflecting our meritocratic team approach.
Required attributes
Minimum 2.1 achieved or predicted from a top-tier university (unless exceptional experience)
Exceptional critical thinking and analytical skills
Strong written and oral communication skills
Proactive and naturally inquisitive temperament
Language skills are highly desirable but not required
Consulting experience not required but any professional experience will be taken into account
APPLY WITH US
How to apply
We would like to understand why you would like to join Cairneagle and the value you will bring.
The process that follows is structured to test your suitability for the role and give us the opportunity to get to know you.
We will assess your numerical and analytical skills via online tests. You will have 7 days to complete the assessment and the overall process for the online tests will conclude on the 1st of November.
Only after that, we will review your complete application (CV, covering letter and online assessment results) and let you know of next steps.
During the interviews, we are interested to learn more about your experience, your motivations and you as a person, while we will further test your suitability for the role. Case studies will help us see how you structure your thinking, deal with intellectual challenges and draw conclusions.
To know more about the interview process please read the relevant section on our Career FAQs tab (under Careers).
