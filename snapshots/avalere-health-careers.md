Recruitment alert
We have been made aware of unauthorized individuals impersonating Avalere Health and approaching industry talent with fraudulent job offers.
Please protect yourself by keeping the following in mind:
Official channels: All legitimate correspondence regarding our open roles will exclusively come from an @avalerehealth.com email address.
No upfront fees: We will never ask for payment, bank details, or personal financial information during the interview process.
Verify openings: You can view all our current, legitimate job openings directly on our official careers page.
If you suspect you’ve been contacted by a scammer, please report it to our security team at security.alerts@avalerehealth.com.
