Cairneagle is growing
A strategy consulting firm with a difference.
Do you have what it takes?
Cairneagle is a fast growing strategy consulting firm with a difference.
We are growing quickly. We rapidly scale our team and seek exceptional candidates to support our continued business growth.
We are award-winning Education specialists. We create innovative solutions for our clients that drive competitive advantage and deliver their educational mission. Education is a wide-ranging, constantly evolving sector. Our clients include educational institutions, suppliers of education products and services, and investors.
We are international. Our projects can cover the UK, Europe, Asia, the Americas, and Africa.
What is it like to work for Cairneagle?
Challenging. Rewarding. Social.
Cairneagle is a fast-paced, high stakes, intellectually demanding working environment. We work in small teams directly with partners, giving unparalleled exposure to sector leaders and accelerating professional development. From day one, team members take on real responsibility and drive value for our clients. Cases are complex. Everyone in the team is encouraged to think about the ‘big picture’ and contribute to the answer.
Cairneagle is a supportive learning environment. Consultants receive continuous feedback during and at the end of each project, and we have formal reviews every 6 months with the opportunity to discuss career progression. We promote based only on ability and pride ourselves on our meritocratic culture.
We are a tight-knit, committed team from diverse cultural backgrounds. Staying healthy, happy and cohesive is vital to our success. We organise regular social events, end of project dinners and have dedicated company off-sites; there is also a well-being programme on offer to all employees.
Who are we looking for?
Self-starters with analytical and curious minds.
The nature of our work requires exceptional critical thinking and analytical skills. Our work blends quantitative skills, structured curiosity, and effective communication. Every contribution counts, the ability to work cohesively and collaboratively is non-negotiable. We never stop learning and never tire of the pursuit of excellence. Finally, we live and breathe a social workplace – we are currently looking for Graduates and Experienced Consultants who can positively impact our team culture.
To explore a future career with us, send us your application.
