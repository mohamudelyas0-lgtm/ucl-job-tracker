What is Strategy?
Start your career in Strategy and you’ll place yourself at the boardroom table, where the biggest decisions are made. We work alongside the world’s leading organisations, helping them to seize advantage and solve their most important challenges. Providing strategic guidance and insights across a broad range of sectors, we give leaders the clarity to make well-informed decisions about the future.
It’s a career path with diverse and challenging assignments. Our teams work with a wide variety of UK, European and Global corporate clients (from large consumer goods companies, rapidly scaling AI-enabled tech platforms to charities, government departments and the public sector), as well as major investors (private equity and investment funds).
We deliver a mix of strategy consulting projects, supporting clients with growth strategy, market entry, mergers and acquisitions, target operating models and value creation. We evaluate each client’s unique proposition and market positioning, identifying innovative opportunities for growth, flagging risks and defining the technology, talent and skills they need for the future.
We analyse market trends, assess business performance and track candidate and consumer behaviours to get a complete picture of the situation.
Next, we help our clients translate our insights into decisive action, developing recommendations that help clients achieve their goals by optimising operations and enhancing performance. To deliver, we combine our strategic advice with the broad capabilities of the PwC network, giving clients access to the expert guidance they need in every situation.
By identifying where to invest and where to pull back, we help businesses to use their time, money and people more effectively. We encourage long-term vision, making our clients stronger and more resilient through sustainable and responsible decision-making.
Strategy is a fast-paced and fascinating place to build your career. You’ll work in diverse teams, full of new ideas and fresh perspectives, using a blend of commercial, analytical, AI, and people skills to solve complex problems and unlock opportunities.
What you'll do
You’ll provide clarity and direction​
Whether your client is a major corporation, a private equity firm or a government department, you’ll help them understand their current position, make the most of their opportunities and move forward with confidence.
You’ll bring the vision to life
You’ll collaborate with industry experts across PwC, drawing on their ideas and experience to challenge conventional thinking and spark thoughtful debate. With an open and entrepreneurial mindset, you’ll find fresher, smarter answers to our clients’ toughest problems.
You’ll collaborate with all kinds of organisations and people. ​
You’ll work closely with clients on interesting and ​varied assignments across a broad range of sectors, organisations and projects. Each client’s situation ​is different, and our approach is always tailored to ​their needs, so you will gain a broad education ​across the commercial world. ​

Strategy business areas
As part of your Strategy application, you'll be asked to choose the business area that most interests you.
After you’ve successfully completed your assessment centre, you’ll be matched to a role that aligns with your interests and ambitions.
Strategy Consulting
Economics Consulting
As a graduate in Strategy Consulting, you’ll work in project teams on a wide range of strategic questions, moving between industries, clients and challenges. You could be researching a new market, evaluating policy options, testing an investment case, analysing competitors, considering the right strategic operating model or helping turn complex findings into clear recommendations for senior decision makers.
You’ll learn to structure ambiguous problems, form a clear point of view and develop communication skills needed to solve complex questions.
Within Strategy Consulting, you'll be able to tell us your preference between Strategy& and Enterprise Strategy before the assessment centre. These roles are both based in London.
Strategy& is PwC’s global strategy consulting business. We work with chief executives, leadership teams and investors on the choices that matter most to their organisations, from defining where to grow and how to compete, to shaping portfolios, pursuing deals and creating long-term value.
Our Enterprise Strategy specialists help government and public sector organisations set priorities, make complex choices and improve outcomes for citizens and service users. The team applies rigorous strategy consulting approaches to challenges shaped by policy, public value, funding constraints and diverse stakeholder needs.
Our assessment and selection process
Apply online
The first thing you'll need to do is complete your online application carefully, following all the instructions.
Online assessment
A series of online assessments that measure your cognitive skills, behaviours, natural preferences and reasoning abilities.
Video interview
The next stage of our assessment process is an on-demand video interview. The questions will focus on how you typically approach your work, for example at school, university or any prior work experience you may have.
Assessment centre
The penultimate stage of our recruitment process is a virtual immersive assessment centre.
Business assessment
The final stage of our recruitment process is an interview with a senior leader from the area that you have applied to. If you applied to Strategy Consulting, this stage will also consist of a series of case study interviews with a senior Strategy& leader.
